Cameras in warning area

Generate a warning map to find cameras inside its footprint.

Unverified capture times remain labelled. Age-only filters keep these cameras; verified-only filters exclude them. Inaccessible images are omitted before numbering.

Refer to the Bureau of Meteorology warning for official warning information. Outages and closures are not attributed to the weather unless their source identifies the cause.

Map and report files are composed in your browser. Weather layers may be rendered by your organisation’s configured ArcGIS print service. Private service settings and edited report text are not saved in this repository.
