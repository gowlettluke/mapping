# Gauge order review — 5 November 2026

The review covers the 70 entries left unconfirmed across 14 captured BoM warning areas in the public-catalogue validation. It adds 67 reference locations and identifies the two Kilcoy instruments as a shared river site. Unconfirmed entries fall from 70 to one. This does not measure the private QFD feed; its ID and location checks still apply.

All existing reference locations and downstream relationships are retained. Each reviewed link has public-source provenance in [the evidence file](../data/gauge-order-reviews.json). Numeric AMTD distances order only the same named watercourse and basin. Published-list links require verified network anchors above and below the interval. Map links are reviewed interpretations of the cited river networks and public station locations; they do not infer junctions or order from latitude alone.

Equivalent-site instruments retain separate readings, classifications, graphs and G numbers. They share only external river-order connections. The two isolated Kilcoy instruments display **same river site**, with no arrow between them. Headwater and tailwater instruments remain distinct and cannot be reversed or merged by list position.

The review corrects the Torrens Creek Township reference basin from the catalogue’s Flinders label to the Cooper/upper Thomson system shown by BoM. The Moogerah headwater reference uses Reynolds Creek, as identified by Seqwater. These affect ordering labels; source readings and flood classifications are preserved.

The builder checks every reviewed station ID against the catalogue and its recorded location within 250 m. Verified provider locations may corroborate an approximate catalogue coordinate within 1 km; they do not move source observations or map markers. Missing, moved or reused IDs cannot inherit the links. Unconfirmed anchors are skipped, and contradictory evidence fails the rebuild rather than publishing a cycle.
